## Design Discovery: The Briefer Orphan

The briefer (Phase 2) generates rich narrative context — chapter flow, character state, motivations, POV — but nothing downstream reads it. Only `02.5-glossary-reconcile.py` touches briefer output. Translate and rewrite both consume parser + glossary but miss the narrative context.

This is a **design smell**: the briefer evolved into a dead-end artifact. It generates semantic information nobody uses.

## Fix: Context Packet (Translation Brief)

Replace the briefer with a **context assembler** that builds one document per chapter containing everything the translator needs:

```
canon/context/ch0NNN.md
  ├── POV & chapter intent
  ├── Character states & relationships (from parser)
  ├── Canonical names appearing in this chapter (from glossary)
  ├── Important terms with translation rules
  ├── Previous chapter carry-over
  └── Active plot threads
```

**One document, not four sources.**
